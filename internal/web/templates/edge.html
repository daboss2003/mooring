Edge routes

Mooring owns the public edge (HTTPS + automatic certificates) and renders the whole edge config from your apps' mooring.yaml. Routes are declared per app under spec.edge.routes — a hostname → an app service:port — and applied on deploy. The edge can never reach the control plane (control-plane ports are refused).

Read-only — edit routes in your app's mooring.yaml (spec.edge.routes) and deploy. This page shows the routes from the last deploy.

DNS-01 plugins

A namespace with dns01 issues its wildcard certificate through its DNS provider's Caddy plugin. A missing plugin is installed in the background and retried on failure; until it is installed, that namespace's subdomains use per-name certificates and a catch-all on it has no certificate.
