Restoring

A backup is encrypted with your master key, so it's safe to download and store off-site — restoring needs that same key.

Mooring state — to recover onto a fresh server, install Mooring with the same encryption_key, then:

mooring restore --from mooring-backup-<id>.mbk

An app data volume — stop the app's containers first, then restore the snapshot into its volume by id:

mooring restore-volume --backup <id> --force

Restoring overwrites live data, so it's a deliberate CLI step (with --force) rather than a one-click button.
